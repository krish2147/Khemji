Before you come

Straight answers.

Is everything vegetarian?

Yes. Every item on our menu is vegetarian, from the Maggi to the Paneer Makhani Pizza.

How late are you open?

1 PM to 1 AM, every day. Late night cold coffee after class or after work is exactly what we're here for.

What does it cost for two?

Two cold coffees and two sandwiches come to about ₹400. Two teas and a plate of fries is under ₹200.

Is it good for a big group?

Yes. There's seating inside, a window bar for two, and tables outside for the whole gang.

Where exactly are you?

Shop 8, Samanway Westfields, on Vasna Bhayli Main Road, opposite Raajpath Complex, Bhayli.
